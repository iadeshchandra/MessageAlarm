Message Alarm is a smart Android app designed for freelancers who can't afford to miss important notifications. Whether it's a new Fiverr order, a client message, or an Upwork Best Match job — Message Alarm wakes your phone with a loud alarm, a full-screen popup showing the notification content, and gives you the option to Stop or Snooze.

Unlike regular notification sounds that are easy to miss, Message Alarm plays a dedicated alarm tone at full volume, vibrates your phone, and lights up your screen — even when it's locked. You choose exactly which apps trigger the alarm, set quiet hours for Do Not Disturb, pick your own alarm sound, and control how many times the alarm repeats.

Built with modern Android architecture (MVVM, Room, Kotlin Coroutines, DataStore, Material Design 3) and fully Play Protect compliant. The APK is automatically built and signed via GitHub Actions — no Android Studio required.
